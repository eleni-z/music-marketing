Why the playbook changed

Reach isn't bought with a single ad anymore — it's built.

The last three years reshaped how music travels from an artist's hard drive to a listener's ears. Short-form video became the primary discovery engine, algorithmic playlists began rewarding engagement signals over industry connections, and audiences started expecting direct, unpolished access to artists rather than press-cycle announcements.

At the same time, a smaller share of deeply invested "superfans" now drives a disproportionate share of revenue — which means the most effective strategies increasingly optimize for loyalty and community, not just impressions.
