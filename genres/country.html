What's working

Three techniques defining country rollouts

📖

Narrative, emotion-first clips

Songs that land as a complete short story in 15–30 seconds — a hook plus a clear emotional turn — are the ones that break out overnight on TikTok.

🔀

Cross-genre crossover singles

Pairing a country act with a pop or hip-hop artist (as with Morgan Wallen & Tate McRae's "What I Want," or Shaboozey's "A Bar Song") opens the door to audiences the genre wouldn't otherwise reach.

🌍

Export-market targeting

With Australia, Canada, and parts of Northern Europe growing fast, country marketing increasingly plans for international playlist and radio push, not just domestic charts.
