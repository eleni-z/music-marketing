Reading these charts

Growth is real — but it's uneven and channel-specific.

No single platform or technique explains 2026's growth on its own. Streaming revenue keeps climbing, short-form video keeps eating discovery, superfans keep concentrating revenue, and AI keeps expanding its footprint in production and curation alike. The through-line: control over direct fan relationships (first-party data, community, superfans) is what separates artists who capture this growth from those who just generate impressions for a platform.
