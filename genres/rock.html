What's working

Three techniques defining rock rollouts

🧭

Sub-genre targeting first

Before any campaign, identify which sub-genre ecosystem the music actually belongs to (pop-punk, alt-rock, metal, indie rock) — reach strategies differ significantly between them.

🎸

Raw, instrument-forward content

Gen Z audiences are gravitating toward unpolished, instrument-driven performance clips — practice room footage and live takes over studio-polished promo.

🎬

Video-first, label-independent growth

Short-form video is now the primary discovery gateway for rock just as it is for every other genre — and it's letting unsigned bands build real audiences without label backing.
