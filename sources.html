Methodology

How we verified what's on this site.

We prioritized primary industry reports and established trade press, cross-checked recurring statistics against multiple independent outlets, and labeled every source by confidence level. Where a figure came from a single outlet, it's marked as such rather than presented with false certainty.

Confidence key
Primary source The organization that produced the data (e.g. IFPI's own report)
Cross-checked The same figure appeared independently across 2+ outlets during research
Single source Used for tactical/contextual detail only, not headline statistics
