What's working

Three techniques defining hip-hop & R&B rollouts

🎥

Visuals as part of the product

A music video or visualizer on release day isn't a bonus — it's treated as a required component of the release itself, not an afterthought.

🪪

Identity-driven content

Every clip, caption, and comment reply becomes part of how an artist's identity is remembered — branding is built cumulatively, not through single campaign moments.

📅

Disciplined release cadence

Regular singles (roughly every 4–6 weeks) keep an artist inside the algorithm's momentum window between larger project rollouts.

Rollout order

The sequencing independent artists follow

1

Optimize streaming

Get the release ready to convert — cover art, metadata, playlist pitch — before pushing traffic to it.

2

Expand on social

Drive discovery with visual-first, identity-driven short-form content across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

3

Build owned channels

Convert engaged viewers into an email/SMS list or community space the artist controls directly.

4

Layer in paid ads

Use paid promotion as a multiplier on what's already proven to convert organically — not as the first move.
